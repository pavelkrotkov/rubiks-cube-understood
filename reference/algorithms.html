Rubik's Cube · Reference

Algorithm Cheat-Sheet

The growing list of memorized sequences, by stage. Orientation throughout: white on the bottom, yellow on top, until the last layer.

Notation reminder in the glossary. A bare letter = quarter turn clockwise (facing that face); ' = counter-clockwise; 2 = half turn.

First layer — corners

Insert a corner (repeat until seated)

The trigger

R U R' U'

Corner staged in the top layer, above its front-right-bottom slot. Repeat until white faces down. Each rep twists the corner one orientation. Also pops a wrongly-seated corner out (do it once). — Lesson 4

Middle layer — edges

Find a top-layer edge with no yellow. Align its front color to the matching center (an upside-down T), then send it left or right by its top color.

Edge goes to the RIGHT slot

Right insertion

U R U' R' U' F' U F

Top edge's up-color matches the right center. — Lesson 5

Edge goes to the LEFT slot

Left insertion (mirror of the above)

U' L' U L U F U' F'

Top edge's up-color matches the left center. To free a stuck middle edge, insert any top edge into its slot, then re-place. — Lesson 5

Last layer — yellow cross (orient edges)

Flip yellow edges up · position ignored

The cross algorithm

F R U R' U' F'

Hold yellow on top. Loop: look at the edge pattern → grip → apply once → re-look and re-grip before the next round. L-shape → bend at back-left · Line → horizontal · Dot → any grip this round. Expect 3 rounds from a dot, 2 from an L, 1 from a line. Note it's the corner trigger R U R' U' conjugated by F … F'. — Lesson 6

Last layer — position the cross edges (permute edges)

Swap the FRONT and LEFT yellow edges

The edge swap

R U R' U R U2 R' U

Yellow cross done but side colors mismatched. Turn U until two edges match their centers. Two adjacent match → hold them at back & right, apply once (swaps front/left). Two opposite match → apply once from anywhere, realign, then handle as adjacent. — Lesson 7

Last layer — position the corners (permute corners)

Cycle three corners · front-right-top stays put

The corner 3-cycle

U R U' L' U R' U' L

Find the one corner already in its correct home (right spot, any twist) and hold it at front-right-top; apply until the other three cycle into place (1–2×). No corner correct → apply once from anywhere first. It's a commutator: A B A' B' with A = U R U', B = L'. — Lesson 8

Last layer — orient the corners (final step)

Twist corners yellow-up · finishes the cube

The corner twister

R' D' R D  (repeat in pairs)

Hold a twisted corner at front-right-top; repeat until its yellow faces up (2 or 4 reps). Then turn only U to bring the next twisted corner into that same spot and repeat. The bottom layers look wrecked mid-process — they self-restore when the last corner is done. Finish by aligning U. Also a commutator: [R', D']. — Lesson 9

That's the complete beginner method — cross, corners, middle edges, yellow cross, edge permute, corner permute, corner orient. None of these sequences is arbitrary: each entry links to the lesson that takes it apart. If one still feels like magic after its lesson, that's a genuinely good "why does this work?" question for Speedsolving.com or r/Cubers.
